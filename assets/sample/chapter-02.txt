This is chapter two. A good listening companion should make it easy to get back to your story. In the morning, perhaps you listen while making coffee. Later, on a walk, the same book is ready in your headphones. Your place belongs to the book, not to a particular screen. The folder list can be browsed without interrupting playback. The large controls can be opened with one tap. If you touch the progress bar by mistake, a checkpoint remembers where you were. Open the history to see your recent listening positions, and restore one when you need it. You can also save a bookmark intentionally. These small details make a player easier to trust. Now try the twenty second controls, switch between the chapter list and car mode, or pause this recording. When you are ready, the final sample chapter will follow.
