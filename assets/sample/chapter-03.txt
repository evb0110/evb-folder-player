You have reached the final sample chapter. To add your own audiobooks, return to the library and choose add folder. Select an audiobook folder on your phone and allow access to its contents. Nested folders are kept in their original structure. You can also scan the device for audio files that Android already knows about. The files remain where they are. EVB Folder Player does not rename or move your books. Once a folder is added, choose a track and press play. Car mode is available whenever you prefer large controls. This sample is only a starting point. Your real library, your favorite narrators, and all the books you plan to listen to are waiting. Thank you for trying EVB Folder Player.
