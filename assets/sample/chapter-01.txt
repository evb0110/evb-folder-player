Welcome to EVB Folder Player. This is a short sample recording, so you can try the controls before adding your own books. Your listening place is saved as you go. Take a moment to explore. Open car mode to find one large play and pause button, with a twenty second rewind on the left and a twenty second jump forward on the right. If you move to the wrong place, undo jump will take you back. Your library can contain many folders, and each book keeps its own listening position. You can leave a bookmark whenever you like. Close the screen, return later, and continue your story. This sample is included with the app and works without the internet. The next chapter begins automatically when this recording ends. Until then, enjoy a quieter moment. There is no hurry. Your next chapter will be here when you are ready.
